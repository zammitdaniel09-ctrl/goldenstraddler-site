Questions people ask

Can't see yours? Ask the assistant, and a person can take over.

Which platforms does it work with?

MetaTrader 5 syncs automatically through the connector. MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5 history reports import in one go, and any platform that exports closed trades as CSV works too, including cTrader and most prop firms.

How does the MT5 connector work?

It's a small Expert Advisor you attach to any chart. It sends your closed trades with the stop loss each one opened with and how far price went for and against it, then each new trade as it closes. It only reads your history: it never places, changes or closes a trade, and it never sees your password.

Do I need the GoldenStraddler EA?

No. The journal works for any trading, manual or automated, on any symbol. If you do run GoldenStraddler, its trades appear in your journal by themselves and the journal is included with your licence.

What does the AI coach cost?

Nothing extra. The plan includes {{AI_CREDITS}} AI credits a month. A question or a trade review uses one credit and a written weekly or monthly review uses four. Credits reset each month.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your journal is private to your account, and the AI coach only sees your own trades when you ask it something. You can export everything as a CSV at any time.

Does it work for prop firm challenges?

Yes. Set your challenge's daily loss limit, maximum drawdown, profit target, minimum trading days and consistency rule on the account, and the journal shows how close you are. It counts closed trades, while your firm also counts open positions, so treat it as a guide.
