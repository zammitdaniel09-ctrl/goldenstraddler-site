      One release, second by second

      The EA reads the week's red-folder USD events from ForexFactory, with MT5's own economic calendar as a backup. Timing comes from your broker's tick clock, so five seconds before means five seconds on the trade server.

      1. −5 s

        Arms

        A buy stop goes 60 points above the ask and a sell stop 60 points below the bid, both sent at the same moment. Each carries a 100-point stop loss. If the spread has widened, both sit at least twice the spread away.

      2. 0 s

        The number drops

        Price breaks one way and fills one order. The other order is deleted the same instant.

      3. +50 pts

        Trailing starts

        Once the trade is 50 points in profit, the stop follows 50 points behind price and only ever tightens.

      4. Exit

        The stop takes it out

        When price turns, the trailing stop closes the trade. If the window is still open, a fresh pair goes on around the new price.

      5. +30 s

        Disarms

        Leftover pending orders are removed. A trade that's still open keeps trailing until its stop closes it.

      These are the defaults. Lot size, distances, stop loss, trailing, the window around each release and the currency are all inputs you can change, and there's an always-on mode if you want it trading between releases too.

      Which brokers does it work with?

      Any MetaTrader 5 broker that offers gold. The ones we use and partner with are listed above. Attach it to your broker's gold symbol, whether that's called XAUUSD, GOLD or something like XAUUSD.r. It works on 2-digit and 3-digit gold quotes and scales its point distances automatically. Raw-spread or ECN accounts with fast execution give the best fills around news.

      How much do I need in my account?

      There's no fixed minimum. What matters is lot size: at the default 0.10 lots, a full 100-point stop loss is about $10 plus slippage and costs. Use the calculator above and pick a size where a full stop is an amount you're comfortable losing many times over.

      Can I try it on a demo account first?

      Yes, and we recommend it. The licence locks to the first account it runs on, and you can move it to your live account straight away from your account page.

      Can I use it on a prop firm account?

      Check your firm's rules first. Many funded-account programmes don't allow trading around news, and breaking that rule can cost you the account or your payouts.

      How often does it trade?

      Only around high-impact USD releases, the red-folder events such as Non-Farm Payrolls, CPI and FOMC. Most weeks have a handful. Between releases it does nothing, by design. If you want it trading all the time there's an always-on mode, but that's a much busier way to trade.

      Does it use a grid or martingale?

      No. Every order is the lot size you set, with its own stop loss. It never adds to a losing trade and never raises the size after a loss. When a trade closes while the window is still open, a fresh pair goes on at the same size.

GoldenStraddler is built and tuned for gold on MT5: both orders leave in the same instant, every price comes from the newest tick, entries and stops move out to twice the live spread when brokers widen it, and point distances scale to your broker's quote. The red-folder calendar loads by itself with two backups, you get a private live dashboard of every trade, new versions, a setup guide and people to help. Whether that's worth it to you is your call, and the {{REFUND_DAYS}}-day money-back guarantee lets you find out.

      If it works, why sell it?

      Fair question. It isn't a money machine: it automates one well-known way of trading the news, and it doesn't win every release. Some releases go nowhere and some reverse into the stop. Selling it pays for development and support. We run it on our own live account and show every closed trade, so you can judge it on the record rather than on promises.

      Do I need a VPS?

      MT5 has to be open and connected at release time. If your computer isn't always on, a Windows VPS close to your broker's servers is the reliable way. Many cost a few euros a month and some brokers offer one free. The guide has a section on setting one up.
