1. How GoldenStraddler trades

GoldenStraddler trades gold (XAUUSD) in the seconds around high-impact US economic releases, the "red-folder" events such as Non-Farm Payrolls, CPI and FOMC decisions. Those releases often move gold sharply, but nobody knows in advance which way. So the EA doesn't guess. It places an order on both sides and lets the release choose.

Step by step, with the default settings

  1. 15 seconds before the release it places a buy stop 60 points above the ask and a sell stop 60 points below the bid, 0.10 lots each. Each order carries a 100-point stop loss and no take profit.
  2. The release hits. Price breaks one way and fills one of the orders. The moment that happens, the other order is deleted, so you're only ever in one trade.
  3. Nothing moves until the trade is 50 points in profit. From then on the stop loss follows 50 points behind price and only ever tightens. It never moves back.
  4. The trailing stop closes the trade when price turns. If the news window is still open, a fresh buy stop and sell stop go on around the new price straight away.
  5. 60 seconds after the release the window closes. Any pending orders are removed. A trade that's still open keeps trailing until its stop closes it.

Where the news comes from

The EA downloads the week's red-folder USD events from ForexFactory's public calendar feed. If that's unavailable, it falls back to MetaTrader 5's own economic calendar, and it keeps a saved copy on disk, so a restart doesn't lose the week. Timing uses your broker's tick clock rather than your computer's clock, so "15 seconds before" means 15 seconds on the trade server.

Between releases it does nothing. That's by design. Most weeks have a handful of red-folder USD events, and the EA only trades around them. If you want it trading all the time, there's an "Always on" mode (section 5), but that's a very different, much busier way of trading.

2. Before you start

Tick these off first. They're the difference between a smooth first release and a frustrating one.

  • MetaTrader 5 for Windows, installed and logged in to your trading account. GoldenStraddler doesn't run on MetaTrader 4. On a Mac, use a Windows VPS (section 8).
  • A broker that offers gold on MT5. Any symbol name works: XAUUSD, GOLD, XAUUSD.r and so on. A raw-spread or ECN account with fast execution gives the best fills around news.
  • Your licence key. It's on your account page at goldenstraddler.com/account and in your licence email. It looks like GS-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX.
  • A plan for keeping MT5 online. The EA only works while MetaTrader 5 is open and connected at release time. A Windows VPS is the reliable way to do that.
  • A demo account, if you want to try it first. The licence locks to the first account it runs on, and you can move it to your live account straight away from your account page.
  • Your prop firm's rules, if you trade a funded account. Many don't allow trading around news.

About lot size. The default is 0.10 lots per order. With most brokers' gold contract (100 ounces per lot), each point (0.01) is worth about $0.10 at 0.10 lots, so the 100-point stop loss risks about $10 plus slippage and costs. Set the size that fits your account before you go live.

3. Install it in five steps

About five minutes, start to finish.

  1. Download the EA.Sign in at goldenstraddler.com/account and click Download GoldenStraddler.ex5. You can download it again any time, for example on a new PC or VPS.
  2. Put the file into MetaTrader 5.In MT5, click File → Open Data Folder. In the folder that opens, go into MQL5, then Experts, and copy GoldenStraddler.ex5 there. Back in MT5, right-click Expert Advisors in the Navigator (Ctrl + N if it's hidden) and choose Refresh. GoldenStraddler appears in the list.
  3. Allow its web address.Click Tools → Options and open the Expert Advisors tab. Tick Allow algorithmic trading and Allow WebRequest for listed URL, then add this address and click OK:

    Type it exactly as shown, starting with https://. Without it the EA can't check its licence or load the news calendar.

  4. Attach it to a gold chart.Open an XAUUSD chart (any timeframe works) and drag GoldenStraddler from the Navigator onto it. In the window that opens:
    • On the Common tab, make sure Allow Algo Trading is ticked.
    • On the Inputs tab, double-click the value next to Licence key and paste your key.
    • Check Lot size per order and change it if you need to. Then click OK.
  5. Turn on Algo Trading.Click Algo Trading in the MT5 toolbar so its icon turns green. Within a few seconds the panel on your chart shows your licence (for example LIFETIME LICENCE at the bottom) and the state WAITING with the time of the next red-folder release. That's it. Leave MT5 running: the EA arms itself 15 seconds before the next release.

7. Your licence, accounts and renewals

One licence, one account

Your licence locks to the first MT5 account it runs on. To use it on a different account, for example moving from demo to live or to a new broker, open your account page and click Move to another MT5 account. Then attach the EA with your key on the new account, and it locks there. The old account stops placing new orders. You can move a licence once every 30 days.

If the internet drops

The EA checks the licence with our server when it starts and then from time to time. A good answer is trusted for 48 hours, so a short outage on our side or yours doesn't stop trading. To keep things fast, the EA never contacts our server during a news window, in the minute before one, or while its orders are working.

Monthly plans

  • Card or wallet: renews by itself every month. Cancel any time with Manage subscription on your account page. It stays active to the end of the month you've paid for.
  • Bank transfer or crypto: doesn't renew by itself. Use Pay next month on your account page. We email you a reminder before it runs out.
  • When a month isn't renewed you get three extra days. After that the EA shows SUBSCRIPTION ENDED, stops placing new orders and removes its pending orders. A trade that's already open keeps its stop and trailing until it closes. Renew and it starts working again on its own.

Lifetime

A lifetime licence has no expiry date and includes all future updates.

Keep your key to yourself. A licence runs on one account. Sharing a key gets it switched off.

8. Running it on a VPS

A Virtual Private Server is a Windows computer in a data centre that stays on around the clock. It's the dependable way to make sure MT5 is running at every release, even when your own computer is off.

What to look for

  • Windows with at least 2 GB of RAM. One MT5 terminal with a couple of charts runs comfortably on that.
  • Close to your broker's servers. Many brokers' MT5 servers are in London, New York or Equinix data centres. Lower latency means better fills around news. Some brokers offer a free or discounted VPS.

Setting it up

  1. Connect to the VPS with Remote Desktop.
  2. Install MetaTrader 5 from your broker and log in.
  3. Follow section 3 on the VPS. Download the EA from your account page in the VPS's browser.
  4. Make sure Windows doesn't sleep or restart for updates at awkward times, and that MT5 starts again if the VPS restarts.
  5. Disconnect from Remote Desktop by closing the window. Don't sign out of Windows, or MT5 closes too.

Run it in one place only. If the same licence runs on your PC and your VPS on the same account, both copies place orders. Remove it from the chart on the machine you're not using.

10. Trading it sensibly

GoldenStraddler is a tool, not a promise. It trades the most volatile seconds of the week, and that cuts both ways.

  • Start on demo. Watch a few releases before you put real money behind it. You can move the licence to your live account straight away.
  • Size small. Pick a lot size where a full stop loss plus slippage is an amount you're comfortable losing, many times over.
  • Expect slippage. Around news, spreads widen and stop orders can fill away from their price. Your broker and your VPS matter a lot.
  • Expect losing releases. Some releases spike and snap back into the stop loss. Some do nothing at all. Judge it over many releases, not one.
  • Know your firm's rules. Many prop firms ban news trading.
  • Keep an eye on it. Check the panel or your dashboard before big releases, especially after a restart or a broker change.
